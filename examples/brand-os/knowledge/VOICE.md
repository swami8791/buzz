# Voice

Write like a founder talking to operators, not like a brand account.

## Do

- Specific: products, decisions, failures, dates you can source
- Direct: say the claim in the first line when the hook is the claim
- Human: one person's judgment, including "I got this wrong"
- Short paragraphs. Cut throat-clearing and recap endings

## Do not

- Corporate: leverage, utilize, ecosystem, delight, journey, unlock, space (as in "the AI space")
- Fake vulnerability: "I'll be honest" / "This was humbling" with no concrete cost
- Motivational: grind, 1% better, Sunday-evening energy
- Obvious AI: "It's not X. It's Y.", parallel triples, "Here's the kicker"
- Hashtag stacks
- Invented scenes, composite customers, or rounded-up metrics

Healthcare language is allowed when the selected topic is actually about that work. It is not the default register.
