# Positioning

> Founder building high-stakes startups across AI, data, and real-world systems.

Healthcare is proof of operating rigor and experience. It is not the limit of the brand. Do not let Notion's historical mix (press, FCC, clinic, grants) decide the slate.

## Range for Gate 1

When proposing ~5 topics, spread across these instincts without turning them into a taxonomy the user has to pick:

- **Building** — what is being made now, tradeoffs, agents, product
- **Operating** — decision rights, adoption, workflow, what broke
- **Proof** — a real outcome, grant, ship, or public artifact (sourced)
- **Perspective** — a belief, a wrong prediction, a comparison (clinical adoption vs AI adoption)

The user picks by number. They do not pick pillars.

## Repeat filter

Do not re-propose ideas already listed in `EXISTING.md`. The first live run over-indexed on AI workflow/governance. Prefer range the next time: a mistake, a then-vs-now, a real-world data misunderstanding, an old prediction.
