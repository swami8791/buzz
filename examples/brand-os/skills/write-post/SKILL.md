# Write post

Audience Analyst and Hook Master are not agents. This sequence is the Writer's job.

## 1. Analyze audience

Answer, briefly:

- Who is this for (role, not demographic theater)?
- What tension do they already have?
- What will they reject if the post is soft or generic?

If the audience is only "people in healthcare" because Notion had healthcare pages, stop and re-aim at operators/founders unless the selected topic is genuinely clinical.

## 2. Generate 5 hooks

Five different openings. Vary the move:

- a specific mistake
- a comparison (e.g. clinical adoption vs AI adoption)
- a then-vs-now
- a belief people treat as obvious that is wrong
- a concrete scene, not a lesson label

Reject: "In a world of…", "Here's the thing:", numbered-lesson promises with no stake, empty "unpopular opinion".

## 3. Select the strongest

Pick one. Say why it is sharper than the other four. The Editor will attack this choice.

## 4. Write

- Opening = the selected hook, not a warm-up paragraph before it
- Body = sourced argument from the research pack
- Close = a consequence or decision, not a CTA to follow for more insights
- Length: a LinkedIn post a busy founder will finish. Cut throat-clearing.

Claims in the post must appear in the research pack as VERIFIED, or LIKELY with hedging. Never UNVERIFIED.
