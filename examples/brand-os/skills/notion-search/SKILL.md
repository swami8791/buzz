# Notion search

Notion is a tool. Search it. Do not run a SOURCE → INSIGHT → CONTENT pipeline as if Notion were a stage in Brand OS.

Use the Notion MCP tools attached to this session. If they are missing, say so and continue with `knowledge/` plus public sources.

## What to search for

- Stories and mistakes (RealTime Clinic, product adoption, grants, shipping)
- Current projects and agent/workflow experiments
- Publications, talks, press — as evidence, not as posts to rewrite
- Claims: dates, names, outcomes. Prefer primary pages over duplicate press-release copies.

## How to use a hit

Do not summarize the page into a LinkedIn post. Extract:

- what happened
- what was hard
- what Nehal would still defend
- what is relevant now (AI, data, real-world systems, operators)

Healthcare pages prove range and rigor. They do not mean the brand is "healthcare founder."

## Cleanup

Do not move, merge, or delete Notion pages from this skill. Mapping and cleanup are out of scope for a brand run.
